# Product interaction contract

A physical verb is useful only when it makes the business decision clearer. Every loop follows:

```text
player question → physical verb → resistance/commitment → immediate response
→ mechanical result → system consequence → next attention decision
```

## Demand

**Question:** Is this signal worth founder attention and acquisition cash?
**Verb:** resisted horizontal swipe / triage.
**Commit:** reject left, qualify right.
**Immediate response:** card trajectory and acquisition cash delta.
**Consequence:** qualified opportunity enters Product; rejected/expired signals disappear.
**Must show:** segment, why the signal exists, acquisition cost/expiry before commitment.

Starter fixture: one signal. Production slice adds a finite channel pool and meaningful trade-offs.

## Product

**Question:** What should I actually build for this qualified opportunity, and how much verification is enough?
**Verb:** semantic assembly into meaningful slots; components should physically fit/snap rather than behave like a form.
**Commit:** verify then ship, or later intentionally ship with risk when that mechanic exists.
**Immediate response:** fit/snap, verified state, visible defect exposure.
**Consequence:** successful shipping creates activation work for Monetisation.

The starter uses selects only as a functional seam. The Product slice must replace them with tactile assembly without changing the semantic actions.

## Monetisation

**Question:** What can this activated customer actually bear?
**Verb:** time a commitment against a visible price-fit region.
**Commit:** lock price.
**Immediate response:** decisive contact and fit/miss feedback.
**Consequence:** a successful contract creates contractual ARR and a future billing/collection schedule.

This is not a slot machine. The player must understand that the band represents customer willingness-to-pay and that ARR is not cash.

## Retention
*(Standby until $\ge 1$ customer account exists)*

**Question:** Which threatened customer deserves intervention now?
**Verb:** aim/prioritise a concrete intervention.
**Consequence:** exposed ARR is protected, conceded or churned at a visible deadline.

## Expansion
*(Standby until $\ge 1$ customer account exists)*

**Question:** What package fits this existing account without creating stupid service cost?
**Verb:** merge/build a package from account needs.
**Consequence:** fitted package creates addon ARR and service load.

## Operations

**Question:** What is actually breaking, and is the risky optimization worth it?
**Verb:** scratch/reveal evidence, diagnose, resolve; high-variance optimizer is explicit opt-in.
**Consequence:** finite Ops capacity changes incidents, strain, rot, reliability or cost.

## Finance

Finance is inspect/compare/confirm, not a reflex test. Show liquid cash, collections, obligations, debt/interest, ownership, VC terms and what the transaction changes before confirmation.
