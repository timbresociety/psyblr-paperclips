# Consolidation record

This pack consolidates `solounicorn-context-v4.zip` with the latest SoloUnicorn engine decisions in the active conversation.

## Included from the original pack

- product premise, six function interaction baselines and founder-first fantasy;
- responsive React/HTML Canvas PWA direction;
- stable outer frame, centre gameplay and persistent alert inbox;
- progressive HUD unlocks, tactile gestures, motion/audio and accessibility;
- pastel ethereal art direction, Structure/Spark references and rarity materials;
- writing, cultural transformation and generated-content safety rules;
- milestone delivery plan, task templates and cost-aware terminal-agent guidance;
- supplied visual references and their interpretation limits.

## Reconciled in this revision

| Conflict in source material | Consolidated authority |
|---|---|
| Universal growth-or-die commitment | Only accepted VC creates growth failure. Bootstrap/debt-only runs have no universal quota. |
| ARR assumed immediately collected | Monthly service accrual, arrears invoice, receivable, collection attempts and cash ledger. |
| Quarter shop freezes simulation | Continuous time through Finance, inspection and post-quarter choice; explicit pause remains available. |
| Finance as a seventh reflex tree | Finance is a cashflow-management domain. Its future management upgrades are disabled until quantified. |
| Relic power should stay bounded to preserve first-run difficulty | Successful repeat founders intentionally keep persistent advantages. Failed runs award no new permanent power. No automatic normalization or cap is added here. |
| Four-term ARR bridge | New, expansion, contraction, churn, eligibility loss and restoration reconcile to ending eligible ARR. |
| “Autonomy” axis | `Automate` is the engine axis. “Autonomous” remains an experience-stage label. |
| Unspecified balance inventory | `engine/` provides a versioned candidate profile, 244 metric definitions, schemas, event rules and reference checks. |

## Deliberately deferred

Specific founder relic catalogue/effects and relic stacking evaluation, quarter-shop catalogue, complete long-term rank catalogue, full customer scheduler integration, broad policy simulation, human playtest and release assets remain future work. Their interfaces are defined so they cannot silently alter accounting or founder-history intent.

## Evidence boundary

The engine reference suite establishes formula lint, types/bounds, seeded replay, ledgers, due order, finite work, strain/rot/Luck mechanics and financing invariants. It does not establish enjoyment, comprehension, full-run win probability, dominant-build absence or the proposed 30–45 minute pacing. Those require integrated simulations and player sessions.
