# SoloUnicorn game-design reasoning contract

Use this before inventing a screen, gesture, rank, event, relic or situation. A polished interface that weakens the decision fails the contract.

## Start with the founder fantasy

Early play is personal execution. Mid-game is choosing what deserves attention. Late-game is configuring an autonomous machine and intervening when its behaviour becomes unreliable. A mechanic earns space when it makes one of these transitions tangible.

Every loop follows:

```text
PLAYER QUESTION → PHYSICAL VERB → RESISTANCE/COMMIT
→ IMMEDIATE RESPONSE → MECHANICAL RESULT → SYSTEM CONSEQUENCE
→ NEXT ATTENTION DECISION
```

A swipe, tap, assembly, aim, merge or scratch is not depth by itself. The player must decide what to pursue, ship, price, save, package, repair, automate or risk. If a generic control communicates the choice more clearly, strengthen the physical affordance or use the control. Never add fake friction.

## Causality is the readability standard

The player should be able to say: “I did X; the system did Y; that changed Z; now I need to decide A.” Show the immediate delta near the action and provide an inspectable event record. Hidden arithmetic is acceptable. Hidden reasons are not.

Do not convert every function into a disconnected reward meter. Demand must create a finite opportunity, Product must activate it, Monetisation must create a contract, service must be earned, billing must occur, collection must happen and Retention/Expansion/Operations must change the future through their actual state.

## Pressure without noise

Pressure comes from finite founder attention, multiple worthwhile queues, cash-versus-growth tradeoffs, deadlines, load/capacity, uncertain but inspectable risk, customer consequences and financing commitments. It does not come from unreadable clutter, modal spam, arbitrary cooldowns or hidden stat taxes.

Use the stable peripheral frame and central current work object. The alert inbox is a known aggregation point. Routine events do not steal focus. Repeat events coalesce their sound, copy and visual effects while preserving their numerical ledger entries.

## Automation arc

Good automation follows:

```text
RELIEF → THROUGHPUT → DEPENDENCY → COORDINATION
→ EXCEPTIONS → CONTEXT ROT → NEW OPERATOR DECISIONS
```

This is strategy, not a punishment loop. A competent architecture can remain sustainable when the founder invests in Operations and controls exposure. Agents perform the underlying work through the same resolver as manual work, with actual costs, finite inputs, error, rework and rot.

## Progression and mastery

Craft, Scale, Automate and Luck have distinct identities. A rank may change information, timing, routing, capacity, available action, recovery or risk. A percentage is acceptable only when it is the simplest expression of an observable change. Show the achievement on the HUD when a rank activates.

Skill trees, quarter strategies, roguelike relics and founder history are separate systems. A build emerges from their combination. The player should be able to identify why a relic or strategy appeared and how it changes the next decision.

Successful founder history is intentionally power-positive. A winner may accumulate enough advantage to bypass some first-run friction. That is the meta-progression fantasy. Evaluate whether the underlying simulation remains comprehensible and whether new players have a fair learning path; do not erase the advantage through normalization or arbitrary stacking caps.

## Risk and Luck

Before commitment, expose stake, possible upside, possible downside, irreversibility and likely future impact. Operations risk cards and Luck routes may be trap-like, but they cannot be secretly catastrophic or guaranteed rewards. Slight positive EV does not mean risk-free or mandatory. The player can reduce Luck exposure even after buying the rank.

## Failure and replay

Mastery combines perception, prioritisation, execution, cash judgment and risk selection. Failure identifies the earliest leading indicator, bottleneck, causal chain, build and counterfactual. It should make the next run more interesting. Success records founder history and relic eligibility once.

## Originality

References teach principles, not assets or executions. Extract the human pattern, map it to a company decision, invent an original SoloUnicorn fiction and write original copy. Do not copy protected characters, logos, game cards, distinctive slogans, posts, lyrics, creator jokes or recognizable screen layouts.
