# SoloUnicorn screen and flow contract

This file specifies player surfaces. `ENGINEERING.md` owns time-state permissions and `engine/event_contract.json` owns economic order.

## Setup

Show the promise, constrained starting cash, first available action, Founder History, sound/motion options and a clear Start action. Do not show the whole metric registry, seven function spreadsheet or full catalogue before the player acts. Explain that successful founders can make later runs easier.

## Active cockpit

The current tactile work object owns the centre. A stable outer frame keeps Valuation, eligible ARR, contractual ARR, Cash, active time, quarter/month and critical obligations readable. Navigation exposes six functions and compact queue/agent status. A persistent alert inbox aggregates unresolved bills, churn threats, expired opportunities, strain, rot and VC deadlines.

The active surface must answer:

- What can I do now?
- What will this action change?
- What is waiting elsewhere?
- Why should I switch attention?
- What did my last action cause?
- Which obligation becomes dangerous first?

The company continues while the founder inspects metrics, opens the Finance surface, buys a rank, routes agents or chooses a post-quarter option. An explicit pause/settings state may freeze the simulation. Routine alerts never steal the centre with a modal, switch functions or interrupt a gesture. Critical cues become more vigorous in the known inbox while retaining a visible text/status equivalent.

## Progressive HUD

Start with a workbench, cash, valuation, ARR, time and the next actionable result. Reveal detail when its system appears:

1. Demand card and finite channel pool;
2. Product capability and activation queue;
3. Monetisation price fit, contracts and billing;
4. Retention health, threats and ARR at risk;
5. Expansion slots, maturity and package fit;
6. Operations capacity, strain, rot, rework and incidents;
7. Finance forecast, receivables, collections, debt, ownership and VC terms;
8. Explanation drawer with L1→L2→L3 causality.

Required metrics remain inspectable before their first lethal consequence. A rank can reveal a better view or automate a workflow, but it cannot hide baseline costs, due dates, interest, dilution, customer health or the fact that cash is not ARR.

## Work functions

| Surface | Centre interaction | Persistent consequence shown |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Resisted swipe/triage through a channel signal | Qualified opportunity, segment, acquisition cash and expiry |
| Product | Semantic assembly, verification and shipping | Capability, activation result, defect exposure and queue |
| Monetisation | Price-fit timing/commitment | Price, conversion, new subscription, MRR and collection date |
| Retention | Aim/prioritise an intervention | Threat deadline, save result, concession or churned ARR |
| Expansion | Merge/build a package for an account | Slot consumed, package fit, addon ARR and service cost |
| Operations | Scratch/reveal evidence, diagnose and resolve | Strain/rot/incident/cost change and explicit risk result |
| Finance | Select, compare and confirm transactions | Cash, recurring burn, due dates, debt, ownership and forecast |

Finance is not a timing QTE. An automated policy can act in the background, but every draw, payment, purchase, term and downside remains a ledger event.

## Skill tree

Show one function and one branch at a time. Every rank displays price, cash remaining, deployment time, prerequisite, exact effect, observable change, recurring cost and downside/strain. The four branches are Craft, Scale, Automate and Luck. Finance management upgrades are disabled until separately specified and tested.

Tier achievements are visual capabilities: batch size for Craft, unit lanes and load for Scale, visible agent execution/upkeep/rot for Automate and a distribution/risk preview for Luck.

## Finance surface

Show liquid cash, contractual and eligible ARR, earned/unbilled revenue, receivables, expected collections, operating cost, burn ratio, forecast shortfall, obligations, debt principal/APR/payment dates, VC milestone, founder ownership and affordable purchases. Always show the result of accepting or declining a transaction before confirmation. A later collection cannot be presented as available cash before its timestamp.

## Quarter and milestone flow

Quarters are timed reporting boundaries, not a pause screen. At a quarter boundary, the engine settles ledgers, due obligations and any accepted VC target in order while the company clock continues. The player receives a compact causal report and may choose a shop/relic/strategy option while the next quarter runs. Effects enter on their declared activation tick. A failed cash obligation or VC mandate cannot be rescued by opening the report.

The report shows:

```text
OPENING ELIGIBLE ARR
+ NEW ARR
+ EXPANSION ARR
- CONTRACTION ARR
- CHURN ARR
- ELIGIBILITY LOSS
+ ELIGIBILITY RESTORE
= ENDING ELIGIBLE ARR

EARNED REVENUE → INVOICED RECEIVABLES → COLLECTED CASH
COGS + OPEX + INTEREST → BURN
DATED OBLIGATIONS + COLLECTIONS → LIQUIDITY RISK
ARR + GROWTH MULTIPLE + CAPITAL QUALITY → VALUATION
```

## Failure, unicorn and continuation

Failure names the first unpaid mandatory obligation or missed accepted VC target, its earliest leading indicator, affected function, causal chain and two counterfactual choices. Preserve seed, versions, active time, quarter, build, cash, ARR, debt, strain/rot and relic history.

The first valid $1B valuation creates one authored milestone beat, records a successful run and awards only the eligible founder-history/relic outcome once. Continue returns to the live company with ordinary costs, churn, strain, rot and financing risk. Reloading cannot award it twice.

## Mobile and accessibility

Mobile portrait is composed around the active work object with thumb-reachable controls and a compact edge/rail for queues and alerts. Desktop uses width for awareness without seven equally weighted tiles. Support landscape, tablet, installed desktop resizing, safe areas and browser zoom. Use semantic labels, keyboard/accessibility equivalents, non-color signals, reduced motion and independent SFX/music/intensity controls.
