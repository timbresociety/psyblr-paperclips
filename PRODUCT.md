# Product Context: Psyblr Paperclips (1-Person AI CEO Terminal)

## Overview
**Psyblr Paperclips** is a modern incremental business simulation game where a single founder builds a multi-billion-dollar AI conglomerate with exactly **1 Human Employee** (the CEO) commanding an autonomous swarm of AI agents.

Inspired by *Universal Paperclips*, *Bloomberg Terminal*, and modern AI developer cockpits (*Linear*, *Cursor*, *Raycast*, *Daytona*), the player balances product velocity, viral marketing, enterprise sales, customer support, technical debt, GPU compute infrastructure, and venture capital financing.

---

## Core Value Proposition & Mechanics

1. **The 1-Person Billion Dollar Company**:
   - The headline rule: The company will NEVER hire human employees. The Employee counter is perpetually **1**.
   - As the company scales, operations are handled by specialized autonomous AI agents (Engineering, Growth, Sales, Support, Operations, Managers, Executives, and Autonomous Subsidiary CEOs).

2. **Five Tiers of Autonomy**:
   - **Level 1 (Manual Grind)**: 100% manual CEO actions (`Vibe Code`, `Post`, `Sell`, `Support`).
   - **Level 2 (Assisted Scripts)**: Basic task runners and semi-automated workflows.
   - **Level 3 (Autonomous Swarms)**: Self-directing agents handle routine pipelines and sprints.
   - **Level 4 (Executive AI Delegation)**: AI VP/Managers oversee autonomous roadmaps and customer growth.
   - **Level 5 (Autonomous Conglomerate)**: Self-sustaining subsidiaries operating with zero CEO intervention.

3. **Core Telemetry Loops**:
   - **Financials**: MRR, ARR, Cash Treasury, Burn Rate, Runway, Gross Margin (95%+), Priced Round Benchmarks.
   - **Engineering & Product**: Build Points (BP), Feature Sprints, Tech Debt accumulation & refactoring, Compute Load (CU) vs Infrastructure Capacity.
   - **Customer & Growth**: Viral Attention, Organic Leads, Closed Whale Accounts, Support Tickets, Trust Index, Churn.
   - **Holding Company**: Spinning out automated subsidiaries into an autonomous corporate conglomerate.

---

## User Persona & Experience Goals
- **Persona**: Technical founders, AI builders, and incremental game enthusiasts.
- **Experience Goals**:
  - **High-Density Cockpit**: Feels like an authentic, live financial/mission-control terminal.
  - **Instantaneous Responsiveness**: High-framerate simulation ticks (100ms), keyboard shortcuts (`⌘K`, `1-8`, `C`, `P`, `S`, `T`), and zero lag.
  - **Tactile Audio-Visual Feedback**: Satisfying clicks, glows, status pulses, and live terminal logs.
